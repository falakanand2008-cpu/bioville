Bioville

Your city runs on a coal plant and dumps its food waste in a landfill. Turn its waste into energy: build biogas digesters, biomass power plants, biofuel refineries and elephant grass farms. Goal: shut down the coal plant and run every bus on biofuel.

The science

Each plant turns a waste stream into something useful, using typical real-world yields.

ProcessYield per tonne
Anaerobic digestion (food waste → biogas)≈250 kWh
Biomass combustion (dry crop waste)≈900 kWh
Cellulosic ethanol (crop waste)≈280 L
Elephant grass farm (per tile)4 t/day

The software

Waste routing: every source–plant pair in range is sorted by distance, then waste is assigned greedily to the nearest plant with spare capacity, so plants compete for the same farms like real ones do.

AI advisor: a search-based planner. It simulates a month for every building type on every empty tile (hundreds of what-if cities), scores each by CO₂ cut plus money saved per dollar spent, and recommends the best.
